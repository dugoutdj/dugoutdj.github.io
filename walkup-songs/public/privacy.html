Dugout DJ Privacy Policy & Support

Effective Date: November 22, 2026

Dugout DJ helps baseball and softball coaches manage batting lineups, AI/voice batter announcements, and walk-up song clips. You can use Dugout DJ locally on your device without creating an account, or optionally sign in with your email address to sync rosters with dugoutdj.com.

1. Information We Collect

  • Email Address (Optional): If you choose to sign in as a coach, we collect your email address to send a one-time sign-in link and associate your synced teams with your account. Your session token is stored securely in the Apple Keychain on your device.
  • Team & Roster Data: Team names, player first/last names, jersey numbers, pronunciation hints, and walk-up song metadata (song title, start offset, and duration) are stored on your device and synced to Dugout DJ servers only if you sign in or generate a parent share link.

2. On-Device Media & Third-Party Playback

  • Apple Music & Local Audio Files: Songs selected from your Apple Music library or imported via the iOS Files picker remain on your device and are never uploaded by the iOS app.
  • Batter Announcements: When announcer mode is enabled, only the batter’s name and jersey number are sent to dugoutdj.com/api/announce to generate and cache the spoken introduction clip.
  • Embedded YouTube Videos: If a walk-up song uses a YouTube link, playback is streamed directly through YouTube’s official embedded iframe player, which is subject to YouTube and Google’s Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

3. Tracking & Advertising

Dugout DJ does not use third-party advertising SDKs, behavioral tracking, or cross-app tracking, and does not sell or rent user data.

4. Account & Data Deletion

You can permanently delete your coach account and all cloud-synced team rosters directly inside the iOS app at any time:

  • Open the upper-right ⋯ menu → Coach Account… → tap Delete Account….
  • This immediately removes your account record, active sessions, and synced teams from our servers and clears local credentials from your device.

You may also request account deletion or ask privacy questions by emailing coach@dugoutdj.com.

5. Support

Need help setting up your lineup, syncing parent selections, or troubleshooting playback? Email coach@dugoutdj.com or visit dugoutdj.com.
